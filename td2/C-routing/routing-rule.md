Twenty questions and a few runs: a batch-specific hypothesis, not an error rate or a ranking.

## Rule

- **Use case and consequence of an error**: SQL revenue questions on `boutique.sqlite`, three uses.
  *Internal dashboard*: an error is seen and corrected the next day, low cost, can wait.
  *Figure shown to the customer*: a wrong figure is published, moderate cost, answer within minutes.
  *Amount paid out* (commission, refund): a wrong value is money lost, high cost, can wait for a review.
- **Eligible models**: a model is eligible on a family only if it was correct on **every** base-batch
  cell of that family. Repetitions do not make a model eligible; they are cited when they show instability.
- **Order of use** (CM2): included capacity of an eligible model, cheapest first → wait for the refill if
  the request can wait (availability measured again at wake-up) → usage-billed route of an eligible model
  within the budget → abstention. A model that failed the family is never used as a fallback.

**Why these checks.** The 2-of-3 fixed panel {gpt-oss-120b, north-mini-code, qwen3.8-27b} never decides a
wrong value on period, product and age-at-a-date in either stored experiment (one abstention, C09 in FULL-1) (16 consensus,
0 wrong, 4 abstentions on the base batch: [`vote-base.md`](../A-quorum/results/vote-base.md)). On C10 and
on C15 C17 C19 it abstains: only the gpt-oss family is right there, so **no quorum of distinct families can
confirm a correct answer**; a quorum built from the models that remain (qwen + dots) would even agree on
the wrong 119410 for C10 (case WC-3, [`wrong-consensus.md`](../B-consensus/wrong-consensus.md)). There the
check is an independent reference, not a vote. North-mini-code is 0/4 on age at a date, so on that family
the fixed panel tolerates no failure of the two others; replacing it by dots-3-note gives the same result
on our data (one abstention in FULL-1 either way, `family-evidence.md`, last table): we keep the declared
panel. The cheap deterministic checks come from the shared errors we observed: a **non-empty check** (the
wrong consensus on 0 of WC-1 and WC-2 was impossible because 82 and 60 order lines match the age filter),
and a **bound check** (a combined filter can only lower the revenue of the same query without that filter).

- **Maximum delay and cost basis**: dashboard ≤ 120 min, wait for a free quota refill; customer ≤ 5 min,
  paid route allowed up to 0.01 USD per request; amount paid out: no deadline, review mandatory.
- **Stop, defer or human review**: abstain when no eligible model is available in time and within budget,
  when the family was never measured, or when the quorum abstains and no reference can be computed.
  Human review for every amount paid out. The answer is the quorum value or the referenced value, never the
  most frequent one.

**What changed from the kit rule.** Eligibility is the set of families measured correct, not a level;
cheaper available models are ruled out by name with their failing cells; the check depends on the use and
the family, not on one `high_impact` flag; and the paid route costs 0.000134 USD, the measured
token envelope, not a fictitious 0.30. **No free model is enough on its own** for age at the order and for
combinations: their checking (an independent reference) is the price to set in TD 3.
